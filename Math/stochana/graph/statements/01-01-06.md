## statement

A family of σ-fields Fs ⊂ Ft ⊂ F (s ≤ t) on (Ω, F) is called a filtration. We set F∞ ∶= σ(⋃t≥0 Ft ). A probability space (Ω, F, (Ft ), P) equipped with a filtration is called a filtered probability space or stochastic basis.
