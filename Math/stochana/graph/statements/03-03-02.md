## statement

If M is a continuous local martingale with localizing sequences {τn }, we may set σn ∶= inf{t ≥ 0 ∶ Mt = n} ∧ n; µn ∶= τn ∧ σn . Since M is continuous σn ↑ ∞ and so µn ↑ ∞. Moreover, since M τn is a continuous martingale and σn is a bounded stopping time it follows from optional sampling that M µn = (M τn )σn is a bounded, hence uniformly integrable martingale. Thus, whenever we are working with continuous local martingales, we may w.l.o.g. assume that the martingale is uniformly integrable after stopping.
