## statement

(Tightness in C(K, S)) Let K and S be metric spaces where K is compact and d S is separable and complete. Let {Xn } be a sequence of random elements in C(K, S). Then Xn → X if and only if the finite-dimensional distributions converge and lim sup E[w(Xn , h) ∧ 1] = 0. (2.5) h→0 n
