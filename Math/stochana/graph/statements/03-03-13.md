## statement

Let M be a bounded continuous martingale starting in zero, and let Vt4 (M, Π) denote the sum of the fourth moments of the increments along the partition Π of [0, t]. Then, lim EVt4 (M, Πn ) = 0. ∣Πn ∣→0
