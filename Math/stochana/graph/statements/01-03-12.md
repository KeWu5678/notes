## statement

Let M be an (Ft )-super-martingale and assume that (Ft ) satisfies the usual conditions. Then M has a right continuous modification if and only if the function t ↦ EMt is right continuous. If the right continuous modification exists it is also a super-martingale and can be chosen to have left limits everywhere.
