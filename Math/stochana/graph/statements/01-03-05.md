## statement

(Doob’s Optional Sampling Theorem) Let (Mt ) be a uniformly integrable rightcontinuous martingale (e.g. M is Lp -bounded for p > 1). Let τ, σ be finite stopping times. Then E[Mτ ∣Fσ ] = Mτ ∧σ and E[Mτ ∣Fσ+ ] = Mτ ∧σ a.s. In particular, the stopped process M τ = (Mt∧τ ) is a martingale. Similar results hold for sub-martingales.
