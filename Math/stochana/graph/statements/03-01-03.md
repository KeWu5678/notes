## statement

Strictly speaking, we should have defined our co-variation processes along a partition Π as Vt2 (X j , X k , Π) = ∑ (X j (ti ∧ t) − X j (ti−1 ∧ t))(X k (ti ∧ t) − X k (ti−1 ∧ t)). ti−1 ,ti ∈Π In other words, when computing quantities like Vt2 (X j , X k , Π) we assume that t ∈ Π. Since we only consider continuous processes, a simple 3ε-argument shows that the two definitions result in the same limit if ∣Π∣ → 0. Our slightly sloppy definition saves us a lot of notation.
