## statement

This example illustrates that pointwise limits of sequences of distribution functions do not need to be distribution functions. In fact, let F be a distribution function and let Fn (x) ∶= F (x + n); Gn (x) ∶= F (x − n). Then, Fn → F̃ ≡ 1 and Gn → G̃ ≡ 0 pointwise, which are obviously not distribution functions.
