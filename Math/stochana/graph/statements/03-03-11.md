## statement

Let M be a continuous local martingale with respect to a filtration that satisfies the usual conditions starting in 0. If M is of bounded variation (that is, Vt (M (ω)) < ∞ a.s. for all t ≥ 0), then P[Mt = 0 for all t ≥ 0] = 1.
