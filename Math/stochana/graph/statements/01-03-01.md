## statement

A stochastic process M = (Mt ) is called a martingale (with respect to (Ft )) if • M is (Ft )-adapted • Mt ∈ L1 (P) for every t ∈ I • E[Mt ∣Fs ] = Ms for all 0 ≤ s ≤ t. The process is called a sub- respectively, super martingale if the equality in the third item is replaced by ≥, respectively, ≤.
