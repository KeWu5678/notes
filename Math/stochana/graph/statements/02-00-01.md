## statement

A stochastic process B = (Bt )t≥0 defined on some probability space (Ω, F, P) is called a (standard) Brownian motion, if i) B0 = 0 a.s. ii) for all n ∈ N and 0 ≤ t0 < t1 < ⋅ ⋅ ⋅ < tn , we have ⎛t1 0 ⎞ ⎜ t2 − t1 ⎟ ⎜ ⎟ (Bt1 − Bt0 , . . . , Btn − Btn−1 ) ∼ N (0, Σt1 ,...,tn ), Σt1 ,...,tn ∶= ⎜ ⎟ ⎜ ⋱ ⎟ ⎜ ⎟ ⎝0 tn − tn−1 ⎠
