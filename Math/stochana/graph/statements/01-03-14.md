## statement

Let M be a super-martingale. • The process (Mt+ ) is integrable and E[Mt+ ∣Ft ] ≤ Mt a.s. If the mapping t ↦ EMt is rightcontinuous, then this inequality is an equality. • The process (Mt+ ) is an (Ft+ )-super-martingale and a martingale, if M is a martingale. Review: Backward martingales
