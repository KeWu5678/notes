## statement

Let A be a process of bounded variation, and let M be a continuous local martingale starting in zero. Then Vt2 (M (ω) + A(ω), Πn ) → ⟨M ⟩t ucp. Thus, we can write ⟨M + A⟩ = ⟨M ⟩.
