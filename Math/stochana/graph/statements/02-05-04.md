## statement

(Blumenthal’s 0-1 law) Let B be a Brownian motion and (Ft ) be its canonical filtration. The σ-field F0+ ∶= ⋂t>0 Ft is trivial in the sense that P(A) ∈ {0, 1} for all A ∈ F0+ .
