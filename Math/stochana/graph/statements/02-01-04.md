## statement

(Existence of Gaussian processes) Let Γ∶I ×I →R be positive, semi-definite and symmetric, i.e. n ∑ Γ(ti , tj )ai aj ≥ 0, Γ(s, t) = Γ(t, s) i,j=1 for all t, s, t1 , ..., tn ∈ I, a1 , ..., an ∈ R and n ∈ N. Then, there exists a probability space (Ω, F, P) and a Gaussian process X on it such that EXt = 0, EXs Xt = Γ(s, t).
