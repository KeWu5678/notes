## statement

(Centsov Tightness Condition) (*) • A sequence of probability measures {µn } on C([0, 1], R) is tight if and only if the following two conditions hold. (i) for all ε > 0 there exists M such that supn µn (∣x(0)∣ > M ) < ε and (ii) for all ε, η > 0 there exists δ > 0 such that supn µn (w(x, δ) ≥ η) < ε where w denotes the modulus of continuity. • Let {X n } be a sequence of continuous stochastic processes on (Ω, F, P) that satisfies the following conditions: – supm E[∣X0m ∣ν ] ∶= M < ∞. – supm E∣Xtm − Xsm ∣α ≤ CT ∣t − s∣1+β for all T > 0 and 0 ≤ s ≤ t ≤ T . Follow the technique of the Kolmogorov-Centsov Theorem to prove that the sequence of probability measures {P ○ (X m )−1 } is tight. 2.3.3 Donsker’s invariance principle Armed with the above results we can now give an alternative proof of Donsker’s invariance principle.
