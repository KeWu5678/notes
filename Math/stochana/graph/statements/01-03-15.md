## statement

(Backwards martingale convergence) Let (nk ) be a sequence of decreasing numbers and (Gnk ) be a decreasing sequence of σ-fields and G∞ ∶= ⋂ Gnk . Let (Xnk ) be a (Gnk )-backwards super-martingale, that is Xnk+1 ≥ E[Xnk ∣Gnk+1 ]. Then the following holds: • The process (Xnk ) converges almost surely to a limit X∞ . • The process (Xnk ) is uniformly integrable if and only if it is L1 bounded. In this case Xnk → X∞ a.s. and in L1 and X∞ ≥ E[Xn0 ∣G∞ ]. • If X is a backwards martingale, then uniform integrability is automatically satisfied and X∞ = E[Xn0 ∣G∞ ]. The uniform integarbility of a backwards super-martingale (Xnk ) is equivalent to its L1 -boundedness. Since E[∣Xnk ∣] = E[Xnk ] + 2E[Xn−k ] ≤ E[Xn0 ] + 2E[Xn−k ]
