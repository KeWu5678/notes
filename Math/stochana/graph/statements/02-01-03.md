## statement

(Daniel-Kolmogorov) Let S be a Polish space equipped with its Borel σ-field S. Let {QJ ∶ J ⊂ I, ∣J∣ < ∞} be a consistent family of finite-dimensional distributions QJ on (S J , S J ). Then, there exists a unique probability measure P on (S I , S I ) that has all the QJ as its finite dimensional distributions.
