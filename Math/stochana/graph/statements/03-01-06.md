## statement

In view of the above, we can define integrals of (e.g. bounded or continuous) functions T with respect to functions of bounded variation. In particular, the integral ∫0 f (t)d⟨X⟩t is well defined for any continuous f ∶ [0, T ] → R if X has continuous quadratic variation.
