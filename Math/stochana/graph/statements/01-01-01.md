## statement

Let X = (Xt )t∈I and Y = (Yt )t∈I be stochastic processes defined on the same probability space. • X and Y are called indistinguishable if P[Xt = Yt for all t ∈ I] = 1. • X and Y are called modifications if P[Xt = Yt ] = 1 for all t ∈ I.
