## statement

Let {Zn } be a sequence of i.i.d. N(0,1) distributed random variables. Then, ∞ Xt ∶= ∑ λn Zn Gn (t), t ∈ [0, 1] n=1 converges uniformly on [0, 1] almost surely and X is a Brownian motion.
