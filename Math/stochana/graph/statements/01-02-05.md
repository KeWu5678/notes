## statement

i) If τ is an optional time and θ ∈ R+ , then τ + θ is optional. ii) The sum, minimum and maximum of stopping times is a stopping time. iii) The sum of optional times is an optional time. iv) The infimum, supremum, limit inferior and limit superior of a sequence of optional times is an optional time. v) The supremum of a sequence of stopping times is a stopping time. vi) If T and S are stopping times and A ∈ FS , then A ∩ {S ≤ T } ∈ FT . In particular, if S ≤ T , then FS ⊂ FT . vii) If T and S are stopping times, then FT ∧S = FT ∩ FS and each of the events {T < S}, {S < T }, {T ≤ S}, {S ≤ T } and {T = S} belongs to FT ∩ FS .
