## statement

(Kolmogorov-Centsov) Let X be a stochastic process on (Ω, F, P) such that E [∣Xt − Xs ∣α ] ≤ C∣t − s∣1+β , 0≤s≤t≤T for some α, β, C > 0. Then there exists a continuous modification X̃ that satisfies ⎛ ∣X̃t (ω) − X̃s (ω)∣ ⎞ P sup γ ≤δ =1 ⎝0<t−s<h(ω) ∣t − s∣ ⎠ β for some δ > 0, where h is a positive random variable and γ ∈ (0, α ).
