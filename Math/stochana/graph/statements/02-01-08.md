## statement

Let E be a complete separable metric space equipped with the Borel-σ-field E = B(E). If I is a countable set, then E I = B(E)I = B(E I ). The above lemma shows that this is not true for uncountable index sets. The intuitive reason is that σ-algebras are only stable w.r.t. countable but not uncountable operations.
