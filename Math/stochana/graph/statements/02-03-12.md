## statement

Let (E, d) be a Polish space and let P(E) be the space of probability measures on E. Let dBL (µ, ν) ∶= sup ∣∫ f dµ − ∫ f dν∣ , f ∈BL1 (E) where BL1 (E) denotes the set of all functions f ∶ E → R such that ∥f ∥∞ ≤ 1 and Lip(f ) ≤ 1. Then dBL metrizes the weak convergence of probability measures.
