## statement

Let {µn } be a sequence of signed measures on [a, b]. The sequence converges weakly to a signed measure µ if and only if supn ∥µn ∥ < ∞ and every subsequence of the sequence of distribution functions {Fµn } contain a further subsequence that converges to Fµ at all points excepting points of an at most countable set.
