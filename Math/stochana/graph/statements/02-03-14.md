## statement

(Sequential compactness on compact metric spaces) Let K be a compact metric space. Then every sequence of probability measures (µn ) ⊂ P(K) admits a subsequence (µnk ) and a probability measure µ ∈ P(K) such that dBL (µnk , µ) → 0. Equivalently, P(K) is weakly sequentially compact.
