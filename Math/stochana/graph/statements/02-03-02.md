## statement

Let P be the probability measure on Ω = C([0, ∞), R) w.r.t. which the canonical process is a Brownian motion. Then P is called the Wiener measure. The triple (Ω, B(C), P) is called the Wiener space. This is the canonical model when working with Brownian motion.
