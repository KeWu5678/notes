## statement

(*) Suppose that (Xt ) is a right-continuous sub-martingale and that σ, τ are two bounded stopping times with σ ≤ τ a.s. Then Xτ and Xσ are integrable and E[Xτ ∣Fσ ] ≥ Xσ .
