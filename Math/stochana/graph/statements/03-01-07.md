## statement

Let X1 , X2 , Y ∶ [0, T ] → R be real-valued functions. If the integrals ∫ Y dXi exists for i = 1, 2, then the integral ∫ Y d(X1 + X2 ) exist and ∫ Y d(X1 + X2 ) = ∫ Y dX1 + ∫ Y dX2 .
