## statement

Let M be a local martingale such that the running maximum Mt∗ ∈ L1 for all t ≥ 0. Then M is a martingale.6 In particular, every bounded local martingale is a martingale. Proof: Let {τn } be a localising sequence and s ≤ t. Then E[Mt∧τn ∣Fs ] = Ms∧τn , Since ∣Mt∧τn ∣ ≤ ∣Mt∗ ∣
