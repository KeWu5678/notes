## statement

• A stochastic process X defined on a filtered probability space (Ω, F, (Ft ), P) is called – progressively measurable with respect to (Ft ) if for all t ∈ I the mapping (ω, s) ↦ Xs (ω) from (Ω × [0, t], Ft ⊗ Bt ) to (R, B) is measurable; – adapted to the filtration (Ft ) if the mapping ω ↦ Xt (ω) from (Ω, Ft ) to (R, B) is measurable for all t ∈ I. • The progressive σ-field on Ω × [0, ∞) generated by a filtration (Ft ) is the smallest σ-field such that all progressive processes (considered as mappings on Ω × [0, ∞)) are measurable. • The predictable σ-field on Ω × [0, ∞) generated by a filtration (Ft ) is the smallest σ-field such that all left-continuous adapted processes (considered as mappings on Ω×[0, ∞)) are measurable.
