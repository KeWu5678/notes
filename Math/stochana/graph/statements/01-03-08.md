## statement

(Martingale convergence) Let M be a right-continuous Lp bounded (sub)martingale where p > 1. Then there exists a random variable M∞ such that E∣M∞ ∣p < ∞ and Mt → M∞ a.s. and in Lp . If p = 1, then almost sure convergence (but not convergence in L1 ) holds if M is continuous.
