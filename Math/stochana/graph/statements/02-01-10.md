## statement

(Borell-Cantelli) Let {An }n∈N be a sequence of events defined on a probability space (Ω, F, P). (i) If ∑n P[An ] < ∞, only finitely many An occur: P[only finitely many An occur] = 1. (ii) If ∑n P[An ] = ∞ and the events A1 , A2 , . . . are independent, infinitely many An occur: P[infinitely many An occur] = 1.
