## statement

Let X, Y ∶ [0, T ] → R be continuous and of continuous quadratic variation with respect to a common sequence of partitions {Πn }. Then the following holds: (i) The quadratic variation ⟨X, Y ⟩ exists and is continuous (along {Πn }) if and only if ⟨X + Y ⟩ exists and is continuous. (ii) ⟨⋅, ⋅⟩ defines a (not necessarily positive semi-definite) bilinear form along {Πn }.
